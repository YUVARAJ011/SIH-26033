## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
The proposed solution is to develop a digital agricultural marketplace that:

- Connects farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Provides transportation, storage, and delivery support.
- Uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) for predicting demand and finding the best delivery routes.

Benefits:

- Helps farmers earn better prices for their products.
- Makes agricultural products more affordable for consumers.
- Reduces middlemen and supply chain costs.
- Minimizes food wastage and delivery delays.

## Proposed Solution
Develop an AI-powered digital agricultural marketplace that directly connects farmers and FPOs with consumers and bulk buyers. The platform provides transparent pricing, easy product listings, secure payments, and transportation support.

It uses AI to predict market demand, suggest suitable prices, match farmers with buyers, and optimize delivery routes. This helps farmers earn better profits, reduces costs for consumers, and minimizes food wastage.

## Technical Approach
Develop a web-based platform with separate interfaces for farmers, buyers, and delivery partners.

The system includes:

- Farmer registration and product listing.
- Buyer search and direct ordering.
- AI-based demand forecasting and price suggestions.
- Smart transportation and route optimization.
- Secure online payments and order tracking.
- Real-time updates on product availability and delivery status.

## Feasibility and Viability
The proposed platform is practical and can be developed using existing web technologies and AI tools. It can start in selected villages or regions and gradually expand to other areas.

Farmers can easily register and sell their products, while consumers and bulk buyers can purchase directly. Partnerships with transport services and storage facilities can make delivery easier.

The platform can generate revenue through small service fees, delivery charges, and business partnerships while providing long-term benefits to farmers and consumers.

## Impact and Benefits
- Economic Impact: Helps farmers earn more by reducing middlemen and extra costs.
- Social Impact: Connects farmers directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Environmental Impact: Reduces food wastage through better storage and transportation.
- Consumer Benefits: Provides fresh agricultural products at affordable prices.
- Market Benefits: Creates a transparent and efficient agricultural marketplace.
- Technology Benefits: Uses AI to predict demand and improve delivery routes.
